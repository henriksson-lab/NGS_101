# DNA microscopy: bounded source-qualified chemistry

Weinstein et al. (2019), **DNA Microscopy: Optics-free Spatio-genetic Imaging by a Stand-Alone Chemical Reaction**, Cell. [Defining DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2019.05.019).

This selected assay measures **RNA transcripts** through DNA intermediates, not genomic-DNA sequence. Its positive wet-preparation boundary is in-situ reverse transcription with beacon/target UMIs, hydrogel-constrained amplification/diffusion and overlap extension that records concatenation events with a separate unique event identifier (UEI). Spatial reconstruction is computed from those event counts; the graph does not predict diffusion, association fidelity or image accuracy.

The operational original is the version-qualified PMC6697087.1 author manuscript: overview90–94 and Methods210–248 (in-situ preparation, library preparation, sequencing, UMI/UEI design).
