# MAESTRO: source-qualified bounded chemistry notes

[Defining DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41551-022-00855-9).

Gydush et al. (2022), **Massively parallel enrichment of low-frequency alleles enables duplex sequencing at low depth**, Nature Biomedical Engineering. The source read here is the associated author manuscript PMC9089460, whose title is *Massively-parallel enrichment of minor alleles for mutational testing via low-depth duplex sequencing*. Its Methods are version-qualified; the final published main text has not been compared. No preprint is used.

The positive wet-preparation boundary is short allele-selective hybrid capture under a stepdown hybridization and lower-temperature wash programme, rather than simply a duplex-consensus software change or a different target panel. No whole Duplex Sequencing recipe is imported by name.

🟢 The first438SNV panel row records chr1:2329646–2329666, alternative site2329660,A→G,+strand, probe `CACATCCGGCGTTCGGCCGA`. This is a source probe body, not an assumed full synthesized biotin/end specification. The model checks exact pairing only for caller-supplied strands. It does not predict allele capture, thermodynamics or mismatch rejection.

🟢 In-house Twist probe-pool production is a separate preparative component: universal flanking sites; an internally biotin-modified forward PCR primer with dU immediately5′of biotin-dT; an unmodified reverse primer bearing BciVI recognition sequence at its3′end; DNA Clean&Concentrator25 cleanup;2ug PCR product in a100ul sequential reaction,40U BciVI37°C60min→10U lambda exonuclease37°C30min/80°C20min→7U USER37°C30min; Oligo Clean&Concentrator purification of short single-stranded biotinylated probes. Exact primers and cut/product geometry were not assigned in this pass. The selected capture graph begins with source probe stocks and does not pretend to model this optional stock manufacture.

🟢 HapMap genomic DNAs are sheared to150bp with CovarisE220/LE220, quantified by PicoGreen and combined at source dilution ratios.20ng sheared DNA is converted using KAPA HyperPrep and custom IDT dual-index duplex UMI adapters. Top/bottom molecular identifiers are source-established, but their exact length, sequence/end geometry and read positions remain unassembled. Extra PCR from200ng library to workable>1ug mass is conditional on insufficient stock; P5/P7 reindexing is conditional on technical replicates. The model does not silently apply either to every sample. Source1ug capture mass remains an explicit unresolved stock/amplification boundary.

🟢 MAESTRO capture uses xGen Hybridization/Wash kit, Universal Blockers and0.75pmol/ul short probes with1ug library. Programme95°C30s;65→50°C in1°C steps every48min;50°C≥4h,total16h; hot washes50°C/lid55°C. After16PCR cycles, a second capture uses half volumes of Cot1, blockers and probes, followed by16PCR cycles. Known source double-capture workup remains at its version-qualified boundary; no extra cleanup is invented. Conventional capture65°C/16h with120nt probes and16+8PCR is a separate comparator, not merged into MAESTRO. Final product is quantified and pooled for HiSeq2500 PE101 orHiSeqX PE151. Run assignment remains unselected.

Diagnostic oligos are separate from preparation: library-conversion ddPCR primers `CACTCTTTCCCTACACGACG` and `AGTTCAGACGTGTGCTCTTC` (canonical Illumina substrings); capture-efficiency target primer `ATGTCCAGGTCATAGCTCC` and probe `/5HEX/-CAACAAACATGCCATCTCCTTCTCCTGA-/ZEN//3IaBkFQ/`. They are not operational PCR/NGS primers by similarity alone. Analytical aliquots, unused genomicDNA, actual capture wash fractions and final sequencing pool are physically distinct. Duplex/noise filtering removes observations computationally, not DNA in a reaction.
