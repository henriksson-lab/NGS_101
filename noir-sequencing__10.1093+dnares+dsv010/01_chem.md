## Explicit operations and experimental branches

The Ion and Illumina graph entries represent independent source preparations; they do not assert an unreported physical split of one clinical sample. Set-specific restriction digestion precedes E.coliDNA ligase/NAD adaptor ligation, with four16→13→10°C incubation cycles. Exact substrate-end/target allocation remains unassembled. The ligation mixture is purified twice with1.2×AMPure. Source purified DNA/beads are resuspended in Q5/region-primer mix and the beads are **physically removed before**10linear cycles. After another1.2×AMPure purification, source PCR mixture receives the DNA and beads are again removed before30cycles. These physical fractions remain separate from computational barcode filtering.

PlatinumTaqHighFidelity is the main final-PCR source choice; its Q5 comparator is not merged with the same recipe. Source cleanup allocation remains explicit: Ion bead/gel options, Illumina twice1×AMPure and a later source sentence recommending gel for Illumina/Q5/KRAS/CTNNB1 preparations. The model records an unassembled choice boundary plus actual physical purification instead of forcing both routes or silently selecting one. Ion acquisition uses OneTouchOT2template emulsionPCR/bead enrichment and ProtonPI200v2/v3; Illumina uses MiSeqsingle-end. Full kit geometry/cycles/primer landing remain unreviewed.

The double-strand-labeling validation is a third independent branch, using heteroduplex calibration input and PyrococcusGB-D/PlatinumTaq kit replacement synthesis at72°C20or30min before30cyclePCR. It bypasses the10cyclelinear route. Newly copied strands are distinct from original mismatched partners; observed mutation-rate changes and NOIR consensus/error filtering do not chemically correct bases or physically discard reads.
