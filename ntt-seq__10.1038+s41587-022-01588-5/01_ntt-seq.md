# NTT-seq — source notes

Defining paper: Stuart et al., *Nature Biotechnology* (2023), [doi:10.1038/s41587-022-01588-5](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41587-022-01588-5).

- 🟢 PMC article, Methods and Extended Data Table 2 were read.
- 🟢 Nanobody–Tn5 fusion proteins recognize primary antibodies and transfer one of six printed 8-nt MEDSA modality barcodes; MEDSB supplies the other Tn5 end.
- 🟢 Extended Data Table 2 prints MEDSA_1 as `TCGTCGGCAGCGTC-GGATTGCT-GCGATCGAGGACGGC-AGATGTGTATAAGAGACAG` and MEDSB as `GTCTCGTGGGCTCGG-AGATGTGTATAAGAGACAG`. The diagram splits these into S5/S7, modality barcode, connector and mosaic end rather than abbreviating the transposome.
- 🟢 Methods (JATS line 100): each barcoded T5 oligo and the single T7 oligo are annealed separately with the "pMENT common oligo" (100 µM each, TE, 95 °C 5 min, 0.2 °C/s to 4 °C); bcMEDS-A and MEDS-B are mixed 1:1 and 6 µl is loaded onto 10 µl nb-Tn5 for 1 h at room temperature. 🟡 The pMENT sequence is not printed in Extended Data Table 2; the drawn `/5Phos/CTGTCTCTTATACACATCT` comes from the cited Amini et al. 2014 corrected Supplementary Table 4. Each adapter is shown annealed only across its terminal 19-nt mosaic end.
- 🟢 Targeting and tagmentation (JATS line 122): primary antibodies overnight at 4 °C in antibody buffer, two washes; 2.5 µl nb-Tn5 per target in 300 mM NaCl high-salt buffer, 1 h at room temperature, two high-salt washes; tagmentation in 50 µl buffer with 10 mM MgCl2, 1 h at 37 °C.
- 🟢 Single cell (JATS line 130): cells are spun down, resuspended in 1× Diluted Nuclei Buffer, and 8 µl cells + 7 µl ATAC buffer replace the 10x transposition mix; the rest follows the 10x scATAC v1.1 protocol. The 10x GEM incubation (CG000209 Rev G lines 2286–2350) is 72 °C 5 min, 98 °C 30 s, then 12 × (98 °C 10 s, 59 °C 30 s, 72 °C 1 min). 🟡 The model assigns gap filling of the transferred MEDSA_1–DNA–MEDSB intermediate to that 72 °C step; NTT's bulk PCR labels its own 72 °C 5-min opening step "gap filling" (line 126), but the single-cell text does not say so. An earlier model drew a separate bulk gap-fill step before droplet loading; no such step is described, and it was removed. With surface proteins, the linear amplification program starts with 40 °C for 5 min (line 130).
- 🟢 Single-cell NTT-seq then follows Chromium single-cell ATAC; the paper prints custom Read 1 and i5 primers.
- 🟢 Reported sequencing is i5 38 bp, i7 8 bp, Read 1 60 bp and Read 2 60 bp; the PBMC run without surface proteins used 50+50 genomic reads.
- 🟡 One representative MEDSA barcode is drawn. Its feature role is modality, not cell identity.
- 🟢 The linked 10x Chromium Next GEM Single Cell ATAC v1.1 guide CG000209 Rev G, Appendix A1 (lines 3729–3829 in the extracted copy), prints the gel-bead P5–N16–partial Read1N oligo and SI-PCR primer scaffolds. The cell barcode and sample index are variable windows. The model now places the SI-PCR forward (partial P5) and P7–N8–partial Read2N reverse primers at their computed sites on the cell-barcoded molecule; Rev G is not shown to be the historical kit revision NTT used.

The schematic represents the chromatin library branch. 🟢 The original Methods (JATS lines 110–112 and 128–136; bioRxiv v1 lines 603–634) also describe an optional TotalSeq-A surface-protein branch: an N9V/poly(dT) bridge oligo with `/3InvdT/` is added to droplet barcoding, short antibody tags are recovered from the SPRI supernatant, and a separate P5/RPI-x PCR makes the tag library. The [author's library-structure page](https://ntt-seq.com/html/nttseq_lib_struct) prints the chromatin branch's custom Read 1 and Index 2 sequencing primers and final duplex. A complete molecule model for the separate antibody-tag product remains open.
