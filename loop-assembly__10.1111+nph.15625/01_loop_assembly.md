## Example design

🟡 Four small neutral parts are released from invented BsaI-flanked precursor circles.
Four receiver examples use the same parts but differ in their future SapI fusion ends.
BsaI digestion and ligation give four level-1 references. SapI then releases those
references for assembly into an even-level receiver. Its outer BsaI sites remain for
a subsequent round, demonstrating the return path without claiming a completed
level-3 experiment. Reused molecular classes do not assert physical copy numbers.

The explicit sequence relations check declared cleavage coordinates, cohesive ends,
reference order and retained future sites. They do not establish experimental yield,
copy number, alternative-product frequencies or biological function.
