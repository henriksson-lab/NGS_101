# GAM references

Source files are not committed. Re-obtain them with:

```sh
python3 tools/get_sources.py --slugs gam__10.1038+nature21411
```

The defining source is PMCID PMC5366070, including its Supplementary Guide. Primary
sequencing data and per-profile metadata are in GEO GSE64881. WGA4 reagent sequences are
proprietary and are not reconstructed.
