# mNET-seq originals

Run `python3 tools/get_sources.py --slugs mnet-seq__10.1016+j.cell.2015.03.027`, then the full source-bundle oligo scraper before reading. Registered originals and their text twins remain under `$CHEM_DATA/sources/`; none are committed.

The defining original is Nojima 2015 Cell, DOI 10.1016/j.cell.2015.03.027 / PMC4410947. The two original attachments are Table S1 (`mmc1.pdf`) and Document S1 article+extended methods/figures (`mmc2.pdf`). The detailed protocol is Nojima 2016 DOI 10.1038/nprot.2016.012 / PMC5152764; an exact author-copy PDF is also registered. Neither human NET-seq 2016 .047 nor PRO-seq 2016 .086 is this source.

Registered manufacturer comparators are Illumina 15004197 Rev. C (March 2011, university mirror) and the current official TruSeq Small RNA sequence-table topic. Their actual versions do not identify the unspecified 2015 study kit revision. GSE60358 metadata is registered; raw reads/archives are outside this audit. See `catalogue/source_reviews/` for source-specific reading bounds and pending linked originals.
