# Drop-scChIP-seq — research notes

## Sources read

- 🟢 Rotem et al. 2019, DOI `10.1038/s41588-019-0424-9`, Supplementary Methods and oligo/data supplements.

## Construct path

🟢 MNase fragments chromatin, immunoprecipitation enriches the chosen histone-mark fraction, and droplet barcode beads assign cellular identity. The target is defined by the antibody used for a run rather than an inline target barcode, unlike Multi-CUT&Tag.
