# Sources

No third-party source files are committed here. Re-obtain them with:

```sh
python3 tools/get_sources.py --slugs change-seq__10.1038+s41587-020-0555-7
```

The catalogue records the defining Nature Biotechnology paper's public Springer
Supplementary Protocol and Supplementary Tables workbook. Downloaded files and their
plain-text derivatives belong under `$CHEM_DATA/sources/`, not in this repository.
