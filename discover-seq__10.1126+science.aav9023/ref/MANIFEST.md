# Sources

No third-party source files are committed here. Re-obtain them with:

```sh
python3 tools/get_sources.py --slugs discover-seq__10.1126+science.aav9023
```

The catalogue records the defining Science author manuscript, the authors' detailed
Nature Protocols article, and Illumina's TruSeq ChIP Sample Preparation Guide. Downloaded
files and extracted text belong under `$CHEM_DATA/sources/`, not in this repository.
