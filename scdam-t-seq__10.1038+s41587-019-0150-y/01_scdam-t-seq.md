## Construct path

🟢 A Dam fusion deposits N6-methyladenine at nearby GATC sites in living cells. Poly(A) RNA is captured on magnetic beads for CEL-seq2, while genomic DNA in the supernatant is digested with DpnI, ligated to DamID adapters and selectively amplified. Plate provenance connects the two libraries; the protocol does not require a shared molecular barcode.
