# Source retrieval

Run `python3 tools/get_sources.py --slugs linnaeus__10.1038+nbt.4124`, then the standard `tools/scrape_primers.py` on its external source directory.

Selected originals are accepted-manuscript PMC5942543, published publisher `41587_2018_BFnbt4124_MOESM11_ESM.pdf` (Table 1, rendered PDF p. 41), and publisher HTML identity/subscription preview. Their exact URLs/hashes are in the external manifest. The manuscript supplement and publisher supplement have distinct hashes; complete equivalence is not assumed. Preprint originals remain excluded. No sources belong in the checkout.
