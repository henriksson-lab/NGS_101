# MATQ-seq source manifest

Run:

```sh
python3 tools/get_sources.py --slugs matq-seq__10.1038+nmeth.4145
```

The fetched supplementary protocol, figures, data tables and extracted text belong under
`$CHEM_DATA/sources/`; none are redistributed here. The publisher main text may require
institutional access, but the complete molecular protocol is openly supplied as a
supplement.
