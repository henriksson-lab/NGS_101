# OS-Seq: bounded source-qualified chemistry

[Defining DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/nbt.1996).

Myllykangas et al. (2011), **Efficient targeted resequencing of human germline and cancer genomes by oligonucleotide-selective sequencing**, Nature Biotechnology29,1024–1027. The operational main source is the version-qualified author manuscript PMC4336783. The actual published supplementary Methods (MOESM13) control the inspected literal oligos/modifications and source molecular-event explanation. Its historical heading *A streamlined and high-throughput targeting approach for human germline and cancer genomes using Oligonucleotide-Selective Sequencing* is retained with the actual printed nbt.1996 DOI; a title difference does not establish a chemistry conflict. Final main comparison remains ordinary unread scope.

The positive preparation boundary is reengineering the flow-cell D-primer lawn into target-specific capture primers, then using genomic-template extension and C-primer bridging before cluster amplification. This is different from in-solution capture/OS-panel selection alone and from modern generic Illumina library construction.

Originals read: associated main Methods68–98 and source explanation34–38; published SI Methods1–5/literal primer tablep2 (original PDFp3 rendered /tmp/osseq22-primer-table.png), including C/D modified primer identities and single-primerPCR. Two originals only; actual probe tables4/5 and program exports acquired but unreviewed. Mandatory scraper32candidates/3files; rendered table and source text verified selected whole oligos. No third original was used to fill gaps by similarity.

🟢 Unindexed source adapter top `CGAGATCTACACTCTTTCCCTACACGACGCTCTTCCGATCxT`; bottom `p-GATCGGAAGAGCGTCGTGTAGGGAAAGAGTGTAGATCTCG`, where `x` is a phosphorothioate bond and `p` is5′phosphate. The top's terminalT is a nucleotide after that bond. Canonical Illumina substrings are imported, not retyped. SourceAdapter derives the antiparallel bottom and Scene verifies pairing/3′T geometry. Unindexed and indexed stocks differ: indexed top contains the common handle, six-base sample index and an extra terminal PS-T; the complementary indexed bottom starts with reverse-complement index plus the paired common handle. The unindexed paired core stops before its terminalT. These different lengths are not normalized into one invented stock.

🟢 Source sample indexes are `TGCTAA` (normal tissue),`AGGTCA` (tumor),`AACCTG` (NA18507 multiplex). They are fixed sample labels, not UMIs; structured features mark both complementary index-bearing segments in the optional stock constructor. Default graph is the unindexed column-probe OSSeq366 reference case, not an assignment of all options to one specimen.

🟢 Library construction:1ugDNA→CovarisE210R500bpmean→50ulend repair withKlenow/T4polymerase/T4PNK,dNTP/ATP,25°C45min→Taq/dATPtailing80ul,72°C15min→FermentasPCRpurification→T4ligase2000U25°C1h with sourceT-overhang adapter→Fermentaspurification→**one** Ad_top amplification primer/PhusionHotStart,20–22cycles→Fermentaspurification/quantification. Full genomic junctions and precise cycle choice remain unassembled. Separate adapter-stock annealing85→20°C then4°C avoids source short hairpins; it does not add a high-temperature step to the ligated genomic preparation.

🟢 The independently prepared GAIIx C/D flow cell has C-primer2-deoxyuridine and D-primer8-oxoguanine plus surface attachment; their historical cluster processing is not replaced by a modern standard-lawn constructor. Column-synthesized target-specific oligos are denatured95°C5min, loaded100nM30ul/lane, annealed96→40°C18min and used as **templates** to extend immobilized D primers. Original probe templates/inactive complementary strands are denatured and washed away, leaving new covalent primer probes. Target-specific40mers are selected genomic recognition sequences, not random molecular identifiers. Their actual complete panel is unreviewed.

🟢 Library aliquot22.5ul40–56.6ng/ul is denatured95°C5min and diluted to30ul; unused library stock stays unresolved. Capture65°C20h onto the modified lawn is followed by v4extension/denaturation/wash of original genomic templates. No fresh free D-primer stock is invented at capture; primers already reside covalently on the input lawn. Subsequent96→40°C18min bridging allows the source12base library tail to hybridize with C, and polymerase extends both C and captured DNA into two surface-bound molecules before bridge amplification/cluster processing. Whole modified-surface intermediates remain unassembled.

OSSeq366 uses GAIIx PE40 andOSSeq11k PE60; Read1 reports captured genomic DNA while Read2 derives from the synthetic primer probe. They are not assumed to be two independent genomic ends. Array-synthesized probe production is a separate source option: uracil-primer amplification/excision yields mature capture stocks, but that whole route is not silently inserted into the column-probe graph. Printed U/2-deoxyU/8oxoG and PS/phosphate modifications are preserved in notes; no source-template update is inferred from canonical sequence similarity.

Historical Illumina surface kit/program details, optional sample-index run layout, actual target-panel membership and complete molecular states remain open. No predecessor origin is curated beyond the explicit technology adaptation to source Illumina surface primers; no recipe is inherited. No source absence or full chemical validation is claimed.
