# MARS-seq2.0 — source note

The complete source audit and oligo transcription are retained in the companion
[MARS-seq / MARS-seq2.0 research note](../mars-seq__10.1126+science.1247651/01_mars-seq.md).

**Defining source:** Keren-Shaul H *et al.* “MARS-seq2.0: an experimental and analytical
pipeline for indexed sorting combined with single-cell RNA sequencing.” *Nature
Protocols* 14, 1841–1862 (2019).
doi:[10.1038/s41596-019-0164-4](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41596-019-0164-4).

The schematic transcribes RT1 and the pool-barcoded ligation adapter from Supplementary
Tables 1 and 2. RT2 and library-PCR primer sequences were not present in the accessible
author files and remain explicit sequence boundaries.
