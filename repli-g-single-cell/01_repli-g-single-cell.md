# REPLI-g Single Cell

## Defining source

🟢 QIAGEN, *REPLI-g Advanced DNA Single Cell Kit Handbook* (HB-1306) and the dedicated *Nextera NGS Library Preparation from DNA/cDNA amplified with REPLI-g Kits* protocol.

## Molecular workflow

🟢 A single cell is lysed, genomic DNA is alkaline-denatured and the reaction is neutralized. Exonuclease-resistant random hexamers prime phi29 DNA polymerase, whose processivity and strand displacement produce a branched population of long genomic copies.

🟢 The official NGS workflow advises taking amplified DNA directly into Nextera tagmentation, followed by indexed PCR. The page therefore treats REPLI-g MDA and Nextera library preparation as two connected but distinct chemistries.

🔴 QIAGEN does not disclose the random-hexamer sequences or formulation. A conventional 2 × 150 run is shown only as a useful Nextera run plan; REPLI-g itself does not prescribe read length.
