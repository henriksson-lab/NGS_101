# Sources

No third-party source files are committed here. Re-obtain the defining article and its
supplementary information with:

```sh
python3 tools/get_sources.py --slugs fast-atac__10.1038+ng.3646
```

The catalogue records the PMC author manuscript (PMCID: PMC5042844). Downloaded files
and extracted text belong under `$CHEM_DATA/sources/`, not in this repository.
