# Sources

Run `python3 tools/get_sources.py --doi 10.7554/eLife.63632` for the original
article, supplements, linked bench protocol and vendor guides. Additional routes
are maintained in `catalogue/extra_sources.tsv`, including CG000338 Rev A,
CG000373 Rev A and the author protocols.io protocol.

Downloads and extracted text remain under `$CHEM_DATA/sources/`, not here.
The source-review ledger records exact locations, independent readings and
unresolved linked-source work. The oligo scraper is a reading aid; its output is
not publication evidence or page content.
