# Source manifest

- Macaulay et al. 2015, DOI `10.1038/nmeth.3370`; obtain article supplements with
  `python3 tools/get_sources.py g-t-seq__10.1038+nmeth.3370`. The original full
  article with Online Methods is available as a coauthor-uploaded PDF, and the
  authors' 2016 detailed protocol is also listed in `catalogue/extra_sources.tsv`.
  Direct downloads may fail in restricted environments; the original PDF is
  readable at its listed source URL. The CaltechAUTHORS supplementary PDF is a
  copy of the publisher figure supplement, not the detailed Methods.
- The 2015 accepted author manuscript with Online Methods is deposited openly on
  Zenodo (record 895966, `https://zenodo.org/records/895966/files/article.pdf?download=1`,
  md5 `677a83c80872328a7dcd32455766ca07`); it was placed by hand in the source bundle
  with a `MANIFEST.tsv` row. The ResearchGate copy returns HTTP 403 to the fetcher.
