Run `python3 tools/get_sources.py --slugs ap-seq__10.1186+s13059-018-1582-2`. Canonical originals and extracted text stay in `/husky/henriksson/NGS101_papers/sources/ap-seq__10.1186+s13059-018-1582-2/`. `MANIFEST.tsv` records exact URLs, identities and failures.
