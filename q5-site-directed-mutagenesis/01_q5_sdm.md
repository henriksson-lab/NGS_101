The intervening plasmid interval is an unknown role, not a measured amplicon length. Exact primer landing coordinates are unavailable to this selected model scope; no missing-disclosure conclusion is made.

KLD phosphorylation and circle sealing retain that intended product order. The constructed endpoint is an intended circular DNA class before biological transformation, recovery and clone screening; it does not prove a correct experimental clone.
