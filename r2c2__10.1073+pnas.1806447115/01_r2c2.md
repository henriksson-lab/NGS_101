## Published oligos (Supplementary Table S3)

- Oligo-dT-smartseq2: `/5Me-isodC/AAGCAGTGGTATCAACGCAGAGTACTTTTTTTTTTTTTTTTTTTTTTTTTTTTTTVN`.
- TSO1–TSO8 share the 5′ prefix `TCGTCGGCAGCGTCAGATGTGTATAAGAGArCAG` and 3′ ending `TGGTrGrGrG`. Their respective intervening strings, with spaces as printed, are `rUGA ArU rUC`, `ACrU CrU GrU`, `CrUC rUG rUA`, `rUAG rUA CrU`, `GGrU CrU rUG`, `ArUA GrU ArU`, `rUCC rUA rUC`, and `CArU rUC GrU`.
- ISPCR: `AAGCAGTGGTATCAACGCAGAGT`.
- Nextera_Primer_A: `AATGATACGGCGACCACCGAGATCTACAC [8bp i5 index] TCGTCGGCAGCGTCAGATG`.
- Lambda_F_ISPCR: `ACTCTGCGTTGATACCACTGCTT AAAGGGATATTTTCGATCGCTTG`.
- Lambda_R_NextA: `ATCTCGGTGGTCGCCGTATCATT TGAGGCTGATGAGTTCCATATTTG`.

The last two primers disclose the splint's terminal assembly overlaps; Table S3 does not print the complete roughly 200-bp lambda amplicon or the historical ONT 1D motor adapters. The eight TSO choices should remain separate sample-index variants, not a single canonical seven-base index.

## Evidence record

- 🟢 Tn5Prime supplies full-length ds cDNA with known ISPCR and Nextera-A terminal primer sites. Reverse transcription carries a 7-nt TSO sample index; PCR adds an 8-nt Nextera-A sample index.
- 🟢 An approximately 200-bp lambda-DNA splint is PCR-amplified with terminal overlaps matching those cDNA ends. NEBuilder HiFi at 55 °C for 60 min circularizes only cDNA carrying both expected ends.
- 🟢 Exonuclease I, III and lambda exonuclease remove non-circular DNA; a 0.8× SPRI cleanup removes material below 500 bp.
- 🟢 Phi29 plus exonuclease-resistant random hexamers performs overnight RCA at 30 °C. A 0.5× SPRI cleanup selects products above 2 kb; T7 endonuclease at 37 °C for 2 h debranches the product.
- 🟢 The SIRV E2 RCA products used the ONT 1D kit and one R9.5 flow cell; single-B-cell products used RAD4 and four R9.5 flow cells. The displayed ligation finish selects the SIRV branch. This is motor-mediated pore entry, not primer-extension sequencing.
- 🟡 The page draws three tandem units to make the topology legible. Copy number and random-hexamer start sites differ molecule by molecule.

## Final construct and readout

The sequenced molecule is a linear, debranched high-molecular-weight DNA concatemer containing tandem complements of one cDNA-plus-splint circle. The ONT raw read is divided at splint occurrences; full and partial repeated subreads are aligned and combined into a consensus. Exact historical ONT adapter sequences were proprietary and are therefore role-labelled rather than fabricated.
