## 1. What it is

A droplet method: cells and **barcoded gel beads** are co-encapsulated in a "Gel bead in
EMulsion" (GEM) on an 8-channel chip; the bead dissolves and releases its oligos, RT
happens inside each droplet, then the emulsion is broken and everything downstream is
bulk. 🟢 (paper, Results; user guide Step 2)

Each bead oligo carries 🟢 (paper; user guide): an Illumina **P7** and **Read 2** sequence,
a **14 bp barcode** drawn from ~750,000 designed sequences, a **10 bp randomer (UMI)**, and
an **anchored 30 bp oligo-dT**. Some early libraries had a **5 bp UMI** (14 libraries,
Supp. Table 1 — including the 68k fresh PBMCs). 🟢

| | New thing here | Where else it turns up |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Cell barcode read as the i7 index** (14 cycles), sample index as i5 — standard Illumina primers only, no custom read primers 🟢 | unique to v1; v2+ put the barcode in Read 1 |
| 2 | **Read 2 is only 10 nt**: it reads the UMI and nothing else; Read 1 (98 nt) is the cDNA 🟢 | inverted relative to Drop-seq / v2, where Read 1 is barcode + UMI |
| 3 | Bead-delivered oligo-dT + [template switching](../ref/concepts/template-switching.md) for full-length cDNA, then **shear + end-repair + A-tail + ligation** to put the P5 side on 🟢 | Drop-seq uses Nextera on cDNA; v2–v4 keep the ligation front end |

The RNA front end is ordinary oligo-dT [reverse transcription](../ref/concepts/reverse-transcription.md)
with a template switch; what is specific to v1 is where the barcodes sit in the reads.

## 2. Oligos — 🔴 no source publishes them

**No v1 oligo sequence is available from any source we accept.** The paper names reagents
only; the supplement has none; the v1 user guide CG00026 Rev B contains no run of 12 or
more bases anywhere. The kit is a sealed commercial product and 10x never published its
v1 oligo table. Earlier revisions of this note reproduced the scg_lib_structs page's
reconstruction (which that page's own author describes as an educational guess); under
current policy that page is inspiration, not evidence, so those sequences are gone from
this note and were never in the model.

### What the guide does establish — 🟢 architecture, not bases

> "primers containing (i) an Illumina P7 and R2 sequence (read 2 sequencing primer),
> (ii) a 14 bp 10x Barcode, (iii) a 10 bp randomer and (iv) a poly-dT primer sequence are
> released" — CG00026 Rev B, "Step 2 – GEM Generation & Barcoding" 🟢

> "P7 and R2 (read 2 primer sequence) are added to the molecules during the GEM
> incubation. P5, a sample index and R1 (read 1 primer sequence) are added during library
> construction via end repair, A-tailing, adaptor ligation and PCR." — same guide,
> "Step 4 – Library Construction" 🟢

That fixes the **order** P7 · barcode(14) · R2 · randomer(10) · poly-dT on the bead, and
the end at which each flow-cell arm enters. It fixes no base.

### What `tools/chromium_v1.py` therefore models

Every sequence-bearing segment is created by the module's `inferred()` helper, so it can
only ever render dotted, and the page carries the three required inferred signals
(preamble caveat, `INFERRED — …` step captions, `<inf>` bases):

| Segment | Bases drawn | Status |
|---|---|---|
| P7 / P7 reverse complement | `illumina.P7`, `illumina.P7_RC` | canonical Illumina constant; that the guide's "Illumina P7" *is* this standard sequence is 🟡 |
| TruSeq Read 2 (and its revcomp on the library) | `illumina.TRUSEQ_READ2` | as above, for the guide's "R2 (read 2 primer sequence)" 🟡 |
| TruSeq Read 1 | `illumina.TRUSEQ_READ1` | as above, for the guide's "R1 (read 1 primer sequence)" 🟡 |
| P5 | `illumina.P5` | as above 🟡 |
| cell barcode, UMI, i5 sample index, insert, poly(A/T) | `B`/`N`/`I`/`X`/`A`/`T` placeholders of the guide's declared lengths | lengths 🟢, bases not applicable |
| template-switch end | 18-nt `N` placeholder | 🔴 — the TSO is not published; no sequence is asserted |

Nothing in the module is a v1-specific base: the constants are the vendor-documented
standard Illumina parts that the guide names by role, and everything v1-specific is a
placeholder. `rt.SMART_HANDLE`, an ISPCR handle, an SI-PCR primer and a stubby TruSeq
Y-adapter duplex would all be plausible, and the earlier note asserted them, but none of
them is published for this kit, so the model draws none of them.

### Kit reagents the guide names — 🟢 names only

| Kit reagent (CG00026 Rev B kit contents) | Role the guide gives it | Sequence |
|---|---|---|
| Single Cell 3′ Gel Beads | carry the barcoded oligo released in the GEM | 🔴 |
| **RT Primer** (lyophilized, 4 µl into the GEM master mix) | not stated; the oligo-dT is on the bead, so this is a separately added oligo of unstated identity | 🔴 |
| cDNA Primer Mix | cDNA amplification; the paper calls them "primers complementary to the switch oligos and sequencing adapters" 🟢 | 🔴 |
| R1 Adaptor Mix | the ligated Read-1-side adaptor | 🔴 (not even its end geometry is given) |
| SI-PCR Primer (one common tube) + Single Cell 3′ Sample Index Plate (10 µl per well) | sample-index PCR; the index is read as i5, and each well "combines 4 different sequences in order to balance across all 4 nucleotides" 🟢 | 🔴 for both, including the 4 sequences per well |

### What can be computed from originals — 🟡 *(computed with `lib/`)*

- Fixed-flank arithmetic from the guide's declared lengths: 70 nt on the P5 side
  (29 + 8 + 33) and 72 nt on the P7 side (34 + 14 + 24) **if** the standard Illumina parts
  are the ones used; the arithmetic is only as good as that 🟡 assumption.
- Barcode orientation (both inputs are originals: the paper's Supplementary Data 2 and
  10x's Cell Ranger whitelist): all **3,388** barcodes in Supplementary Data 2 occur **as
  written** in `737K-april-2014_rc.txt` (0 as reverse complement); the whitelist holds
  **737,280** 14-nt entries, matching the paper's "~750,000" 🟢. The i7 read copies the
  cDNA-sense strand, i.e. the reverse complement of the barcode as synthesised on the
  bead — consistent with the whitelist file's `_rc` name. 🟡

## 3. Step by step

Conditions 🟢 from the paper's methods unless marked *(guide)* = v1 user guide CG00026 Rev B.

1. **GEM generation.** Cells, master mix (RT Reagent Mix, RT Primer, RNase Inhibitor,
   Additive A, RT Enzyme) and gel beads are combined in oil on the chip; ~100,000 GEMs per
   channel per ~6-min run, at a ~80 % gel-bead fill rate *(paper)*. The bead dissolves and
   releases its oligos; cells lyse.
2. **GEM-RT**: 55 °C 2 h, 85 °C 5 min, 4 °C hold. The oligo-dT primes on the poly(A) tail;
   at the mRNA 5′ end MMLV-type RT adds untemplated C's and switches to the TSO 🟡
   (mechanism: [template switching](../ref/concepts/template-switching.md); the paper only
   says each cDNA "ends with a template switching oligo at the 3′ end"; the enzyme is not
   named 🔴). First strand, 5′→3′:
   `P7 · CB · R2 · UMI · T30VN · <cDNA, antisense> · CCC · TSO′` 🟡
3. **Break GEMs, clean up**: Recovery Agent *(guide)*, DynaBeads MyOne Silane, then 0.6× SPRIselect.
4. **cDNA PCR** (cDNA Primer Mix + Amplification Master Mix + cDNA Additive *(guide)*):
   98 °C 3 min; 14 × (98 °C 15 s, 67 °C 20 s, 72 °C 1 min); 72 °C 1 min. The guide scales
   cycles with targeted cell recovery: 14 (< 2,000 cells), 12 (2,000–6,000), 10 (> 6,000).
   0.6× SPRIselect. Product, at role level only 🟡:
   `template-switch handle · <cDNA> · polyA · UMI · R2′ · CB′ · P7′`.
5. **Shear** to ~200 bp (Covaris M220). Double-sided SPRI size selection *(guide)*: 30 µl
   SPRI to 50 µl (keep supernatant), then +10 µl SPRI to 75 µl of it (keep beads).
6. **End repair and A-tailing** *(guide)*: 20 °C 30 min, 65 °C 30 min.
7. **Adapter ligation** of the R1 Adaptor Mix *(guide)*: 20 °C 15 min; two SPRI cleanups
   (88 µl to 110 µl, then 50 µl to 50 µl).
   Only 3′-end fragments (`… polyA · UMI · R2′ · CB′ · P7′`) carry P7; the 5′-end
   (template-switch) and internal fragments have no P7 site and cannot form a P5…P7
   molecule 🟡. Whether the cDNA-PCR primers' 5′ ends are blocked, so that the adaptor
   ligates only to the sheared end, is not addressed by any source 🔴. The adaptor's end
   geometry (fork, overhang, polarity, bases) is likewise unpublished 🔴.
8. **Sample index PCR** *(guide)*: 60 µl mix (Amplification Master Mix + 2 µl SI-PCR
   Primer) + 30 µl ligated DNA + **10 µl Sample Index** from the plate; 98 °C 45 s;
   10 × (98 °C 20 s, 60 °C 30 s, 72 °C 20 s); 72 °C 1 min. Each sample index is a
   **mix of 4 different sequences** for base balance *(guide)*; the 4 sequences per set are
   not given 🔴. Two SPRI cleanups (100 µl to 100 µl, then 50 µl to 50 µl).
9. **QC / quantify**: Bioanalyzer *(guide)*, KAPA qPCR.

## 4. Final library — 🟢 order from the guide's final-library figure, 🔴 bases

Top strand, 5′→3′:

```
5'- P5 · i5 (8, sample index) · TruSeq Read 1 (33) · <cDNA, sense, ~3' end> · B · (A)30 · UMI (10) · revcomp(TruSeq Read 2) (34) · revcomp(CB) (14) · revcomp(P7) (24) -3'
```

Agreements and disagreements with the primary sources:

- 🟢 **Agrees:** the guide's final-library figure, drawn P7→P5, is `P7 · 10x Barcode · Read 2 ·
  Randomer · cDNA · Read 1 · Sample Index · P5` — the same order as the line above read
  backwards.
- 🟢 **Agrees:** P7 + Read 2 come from the bead during GEM-RT; P5, Read 1 and the sample index
  are added by end repair, A-tailing, adapter ligation and PCR (guide; paper methods
  list the same four library steps).
- 🟡 **Simplification in the drawing:** one 8-nt i5 per library, whereas each kit well
  "combines 4 different sequences" 🟢 whose bases are unpublished 🔴.
- 🟡 Fixed flanks *(computed)*: 70 nt on the P5 side (29 + 8 + 33), 72 nt on the P7 side
  (34 + 14 + 24), plus UMI and ~30-nt poly(A).

## 5. Sequencing

🟢 Paper: NextSeq 500, 2 × 75 kit, loaded at 2.1 pM, **98 bp Read 1, 14 bp I7, 8 bp I5,
10 bp Read 2** (5 bp Read 2 for the 14 early libraries with 5-nt UMI). Supp. Table 1 also
lists libraries run on **HiSeq 2500 Rapid Run V2**, which the methods text does not mention.
🟢 Guide: same read lengths ("any other length will result in a failed run" for I7, I5 and
Read 2); validated on NextSeq 500 (V2, 150-cycle) and HiSeq 2500 Rapid Run (200-cycle);
**standard Illumina primers only, no custom primers**.

| Read | Primer (standard Illumina, 🟢 by the guide's "no custom primers"; the exact constant 🟡) | Reads | Length 🟢 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Read 1 | `illumina.TRUSEQ_READ1` | cDNA, sense strand, from the sheared end towards the poly(A) | 98 |
| Index 1 (i7) | `illumina.INDEX1_PRIMER` | 14-nt cell barcode (as the whitelist writes it) | 14 |
| Index 2 (i5) | `illumina.INDEX2_PRIMER_RC` | 8-nt sample index | 8 |
| Read 2 | `illumina.TRUSEQ_READ2` | 10-nt UMI (then poly(T), not sequenced) | 10 |

## 6. Open questions

- 🔴 Every oligo sequence. The paper, its supplement and the v1 user guide give none,
  and no other source we accept holds them. The TSO is the clearest gap: nothing
  establishes even its handle, so the model draws an 18-nt placeholder.
- 🔴 What exactly "RT Primer" (the separately added oligo in the GEM master mix) is.
- 🔴 The four sequences of each Single Cell 3′ Sample Index set, and which strand/end the
  common "SI-PCR Primer" covers.
- 🔴 Whether the cDNA-PCR primers are 5′-blocked to keep the adaptor off the non-sheared
  ends, and the R1 Adaptor Mix's end geometry.
- 🔴 The RT enzyme, and the composition of the 5-nt-UMI bead oligo (shorter oligo, or
  same oligo read for only 5 cycles?).

## 7. How this note was made (tool evaluation)

`tools/get_sources.py` fetched the Europe PMC XML and all supplements (twice: from the
EPMC zip and from Springer, identical md5), plus the scg mirror of the 10x v1 user guide
and the Cell Ranger whitelist. The saved PMC HTML page is a reCAPTCHA interstitial, and
`tools/doctext.py` made no text twin of the XML, so the full text was converted by hand to
`ncomms14049_PMC5241818.xml.txt`. The guide and whitelist copies that carry an `upstream_`
filename were downloaded by hand earlier and are byte-identical to the `scg_*` mirror rows
in `MANIFEST.tsv`; they are vendor originals despite the filename. The supplementary tables
are images in the PDF, so Supp. Table 1 was read from a rendering.
`tools/scrape_primers.py` found only ddPCR probes and 14-nt cell barcodes in the originals
— correctly showing that no acceptable source holds a library oligo sequence.
