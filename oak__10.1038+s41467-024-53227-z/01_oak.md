## Construct path

🟢 OAK stands for overloading and unpacking. In the RNA-only route, fixed cells enter overloaded Chromium droplets before reverse transcription. In the paired Multiome route, fixed nuclei undergo **bulk Tn5 transposition before GEM loading**; subsequent GEM reactions barcode RNA and tagged chromatin. The emulsion is broken without destroying the fixed carriers; cells or nuclei are mixed and redistributed into aliquots, where PCR introduces an aliquot-specific second index. The two identifiers together resolve cells that shared a droplet. The prior graph placed Tn5 after GEM RT; that was a repository modeling error, now corrected.

## Library-preparation steps (round 2026-10-17-breadth-first-14)

🟢 PMC11499997 JATS Methods (`.xml.txt` lines 185–205): RNA-only cells are reverse transcribed in droplets at 53 °C for 45 min, recovered with 125 µL recovery agent, washed twice in 3X SSC at 650 g, aliquoted (about 4,000 cells per aliquot) and frozen at −80 °C. Aliquots are heated to 80 °C for 5 min and first-strand cDNA is purified with Dynabeads Silane Viral NA (35 µL elution) before cDNA PCR with the TSO recognition and aliquot-index primers. Fragmentation, end repair/A-tailing and adapter ligation follow the standard Chromium protocol (3′ Library Kit v3 PN-1000095), then library PCR uses partial P5 plus the i7 index primer.

🟢 Multiome: 0.3% formaldehyde-fixed nuclei; 15 µL TDE1 (3 µL) / ATAC Buffer B (7 µL) reactions of 12,000 nuclei at 37 °C for 1 h; pooled and loaded on one channel; GEM barcoding and quenching by the standard Multiome protocol; same recovery/aliquoting. After the 80 °C release and silane purification, both modalities are pre-amplified for 10 cycles with 4 µL 10x pre-amp primers (PN 20002714) in NEBNext High-Fidelity 2X Master Mix, cleaned with 1.6X SPRI (40 µL EB). 10 µL goes to an ATAC PCR with 0.6 µL 100 µM partial P5 and 2.5 µL 10 µM 10x Sample Index N (PN 1000212): 98 °C 30 s, n × (98 °C 10 s, 67 °C 30 s, 72 °C 20 s), 72 °C 2 min, followed by double-sided size selection. The RNA library is made as in OAK scRNA-Seq. ATAC sub-libraries carry the aliquot identity in i7.
