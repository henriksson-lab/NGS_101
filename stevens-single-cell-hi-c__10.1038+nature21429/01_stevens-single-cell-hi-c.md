# Stevens single-cell Hi-C

## 1. What it is

A single-cell Hi-C workflow in which nuclei are isolated before their individual restriction, ligation and amplification reactions.

## 2. Sources and evidence

- 🟢 Stevens et al., *Nature* 2017, DOI [10.1038/nature21429](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature21429), defining paper and methods.
- 🟢 The reported protocol uses AluI, a blunt cutter, and biotin marking before ligation and enrichment.
- 🟡 The final TruSeq arm bases are inferred from the named Illumina workflow.

## 3. Distinguishing chemistry

The essential distinction from Nagano 2013 is the early isolation of individual nuclei and the blunt AluI restriction boundary. The page therefore does not draw a GATC cohesive-end fill junction.
