## Evidence used

- 🟢 λ filler DNA is added before immunoprecipitation to permit 1–10-ng inputs (abstract); spiked-in *A. thaliana* DNA is part of the workflow (Fig. 2 legend).
- 🟢 Library preparation precedes immunoprecipitation, which precedes amplification (Fig. 2 legend, Steps 24–156).
- 🟢 Kapa Hyper end repair and A-tailing (Step 26), NEBNext adaptor ligation (Steps 28–29), USER cleavage of the adaptor (Step 42), λ filler to 100 ng (Step 45), 0.3 ng each of methylated and unmethylated *A. thaliana* spike-in (Step 48), heat denaturation at 95 °C for 10 min with snap-cooling (Steps 51–54), MeDIP, then indexing PCR with a per-sample, qPCR-determined cycle number (Steps 124–131). The graph follows this order.
- 🟢 After denaturation 10 % is set aside as the input control (IC). The IP sample is captured with the 5-mC antibody and Magbeads, washed, eluted with IP-EB and purified with the IPure Kit v2. The IC is purified side by side and serves as the qPCR reference (Steps 62–128). Step 158 says IC libraries can also be sequenced; the graph records the IC as a QC terminal and does not draw a separate IC library.
- 🟢 After PCR, the library gets a 1× AMPure cleanup and a 0.6×/1.2× AMPure dual size selection (Steps 132–156). The graph draws both with their discarded fractions.
- 🟢 Filler DNA is not adapter-ligated, so it does not contribute reads (lines 342–345).
- 🟢 Sequencing: the Procedure (Step 158) specifies paired-end 75-bp reads with at least 30 million reads per IP sample; the 'Sequencing' section (lines 542–556) reports single-end or paired-end 50–125-bp reads on HiSeq/NextSeq for the authors' runs. The two passages describe a recommendation and a range, not a conflict. The page's dual-index read model remains a platform shell.
- 🟡 The defining [Nature Protocols article](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41596-019-0202-2) shows sequencing adaptors in Fig. 2 and names the NEBNext Multiplex Oligos kit, but it prints no adaptor or primer sequence. The genomic library shell is therefore a platform model. The page caveat calls it an INFERRED platform shell, and the adapter input, shell segments and PCR primers are all marked inferred. This stays until the NEB vendor oligo manual for the specified kit is reviewed.

## Protocol boundary

cfMeDIP-seq is an enrichment assay and does not infer single-base methylation as bisulfite or enzymatic-conversion libraries do.

## Open questions

- Adapter and primer sequences: the article names the NEBNext Multiplex Oligos for Illumina kit (E7335L) but prints no sequences, and the kit revision used is not stated. Until the NEB vendor original is reviewed, the page marks the adapters and PCR primers as inferred. The PCR cycle number is set per sample by qPCR (Steps 124–131) and is not one fixed value.
- Diagenode MagMeDIP kit manual (antibody/IP buffer conditions, kit spike-in controls): deferred vendor original.
- QC1 primer pairs for the *A. thaliana* spike-ins are kit-supplied and not printed (Step 117).
- Spike-in *A. thaliana* DNA sequences: supplied in the Diagenode MagMeDIP Kit / DNA methylation control package, not printed in the article. λ filler primers are printed (Table 1) but not modelled, because filler gives no reads; the details of filler PCR and M.SssI methylation (Steps 1–21) were not curated.
- Shen et al. 2018 Nature (10.1038/s41586-018-0703-0) predecessor Methods: deferred; predecessor values would not establish the 2019 protocol's choices.
