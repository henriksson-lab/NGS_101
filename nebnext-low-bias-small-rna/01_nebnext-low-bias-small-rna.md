## Native chemistry and physical recoveries

🟢 Eligible RNA has a 5′ phosphate, 3′ hydroxyl and length below 120 nt. After denaturation and native 3′-adapter ligation, the removal enzyme processes excess adapter. These chemical products remain molecular classes until a physical separation; they are not labelled discarded merely because enzymatic processing occurred.

🟢 Figure 1 states that 5′-adapter ligation and enzymatic modification of the previously ligated 3′ adapter occur simultaneously, generating the RT primer. The source-derived priming component is represented without asserting a free versus tethered native topology. RT creates an antisense DNA-copy class beside the retained RNA-template class; their exact native adapter/primer connectivity remains unassigned.

🟢 The RT selection first binds large fragments with 1X beads, **retains the supernatant** and discards those beads (§6.6). A second 1X bead addition plus isopropanol retains bound cDNA, discarding supernatant and washes (§6.13 onward). This actual native precursor enters UDI PCR; the endpoint is not used as the template.

🟢 Selected post-PCR branch 8A discards large-fragment 1.2X beads while retaining supernatant, then adds 0.8X beads to recover the short library. The alternative 8B 2X broad cleanup remains a separate route, rather than a composite operation in the same reaction.
