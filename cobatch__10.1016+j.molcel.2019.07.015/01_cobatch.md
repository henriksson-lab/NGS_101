# CoBATCH — research notes

Defining source: Wang et al., 2019, [doi:10.1016/j.molcel.2019.07.015](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.molcel.2019.07.015).

## Sources read

- 🟢 Full preprint/article Methods: antibody targeting, PAT binding, barcoded tagmentation, pooling, redistribution and indexed PCR.
- 🟢 Supplementary oligo workbook: Mosaic-A/B, phosphorylated common ME oligo, PAT-T5 and PAT-T7 barcode series, Nextera i5/i7 PCR primers.

## Molecular path

🟢 Antibody-bound Protein-A–Tn5 deposits barcoded adapters at target chromatin in a first plate. The cells are pooled and redistributed; i5/i7 PCR indexes encode the second plate. A cell is identified by the combined T5, T7, i5 and i7 values.

🟢 The published barcode tables are whitelists. The schematic displays representative barcode windows while canonicalizing only the invariant Nextera sequences through the shared model.
