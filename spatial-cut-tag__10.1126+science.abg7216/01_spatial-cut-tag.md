# Spatial-CUT&Tag — source notes

Defining paper: Deng et al., *Science* (2022), [doi:10.1126/science.abg7216](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.abg7216).

- 🟢 PMC article and accessible preprint Methods were read.
- 🟢 Antibody-directed pA–Tn5 tagmentation occurs in a fixed tissue section.
- 🟢 Parallel microchannels ligate barcode A; a perpendicular channel array then ligates barcode B. The ordered A×B combination is the spatial pixel.
- 🟢 DNA is collected after crosslink reversal and completed as a sequencing library.
- 🔴 Complete adapter and barcode oligonucleotide tables were not present in the accessible source bundle. The page therefore marks outer adapter geometry inferred and does not invent exact barcode bases.
