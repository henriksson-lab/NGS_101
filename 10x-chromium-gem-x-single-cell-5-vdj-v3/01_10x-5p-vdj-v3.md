## Independent migration review correction (2026-10-18)

The original migration retained the same amplified, barcode-bearing duplex as both
RT output and template-switching output. This incorrectly placed the bead-derived
barcode and UMI on the molecule before the bead TSO had been consumed. CG000734
Rev A pp.17–18 (text lines 1040–1106) instead identifies the bead oligo as the TSO
and explicitly describes the recovered molecules as first-strand cDNA before
amplification. Its Appendix p.111 (lines 6520–6544) prints the bead sequence.

The graph now distinguishes an unbarcoded first-strand cDNA with an unassigned
Primer B end from the switched single-stranded product. The latter copies only
the known bead sequence, in the native first strand's reverse-complement orientation;
cell/UMI features follow that copy. The unknown Primer B end is not filled from the
predecessor PN-2000007 reagent.

The early cDNA PCR now consumes that actual switched strand. PN-2000089's two
primer identities are verified against CG000331 Rev E p.72 (text lines 6814–6838),
including the 22-base reverse primer. Their identities do not establish a reverse
binding site on an unassigned Primer B-derived end. That geometry remains
explicitly unavailable; the expected amplified duplex is drawn only as the PCR
output and never used to prove its own pre-PCR substrate. The separate public
amplification panel uses the same incoming strand and limitation.
