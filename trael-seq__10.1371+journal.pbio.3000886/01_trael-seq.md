## Construct path

🟢 A 5′-adenylated hairpin adapter is ligated to native DNA 3′-OH ends with truncated RNA ligase in the absence of ATP. It contains eight random bases, internal biotin and uracils. Bst polymerase fill/displacement stabilizes the junction. After sonication and second-adapter ligation, streptavidin capture and USER cleavage release end-derived molecules for PCR. The page abbreviates the internal stem of adapter 1 in raw display but retains its published modifications and role.
