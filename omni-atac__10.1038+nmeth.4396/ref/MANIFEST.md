# Sources

No third-party source files are committed here. Re-obtain the defining article and
supplementary protocol with:

```sh
python3 tools/get_sources.py --slugs omni-atac__10.1038+nmeth.4396
```

The catalogue records the PMC author manuscript and the Corces laboratory's copy of
Supplementary Protocol 1. Downloaded files and extracted text belong under
`$CHEM_DATA/sources/`, not in this repository.
