🟢 MPX records local neighborhoods of antibody–oligonucleotide conjugates (AOCs) through two successive RCA-generated DNA-pixel contacts. Each pixel has repeated copies of a unique pixel identifier (UPI). Gap-fill ligation incorporates the contacting pixel identifier onto AOC reporters. A-pixel degradation separates the two rounds. The final reporter concept has an AOC UMI, a protein-identity barcode and two UPIs. A UPI labels a molecular neighborhood; it is not a preassigned cell barcode. Computational network reconstruction, cell assignment and filtering do not physically remove DNA from the preparation.
