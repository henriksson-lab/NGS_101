# DR-seq — research notes

## Sources read

- 🟢 Dey et al. 2015, DOI `10.1038/nbt.3129`, PMC full text and Supplementary Methods.
- 🟢 Teichlab DR-seq reconstruction; the MALBAC paper was checked for the common amplification mechanism.

## Protocol boundary

🟢 DR-seq is defined by co-amplification, not a physical RNA/DNA split. Poly(A)-primed cDNA and genomic DNA acquire the common quasilinear-amplification handle in one tube. 🟢 The amplified pool is aliquoted only afterward: one aliquot is processed as Nextera genomic DNA, while the other uses transcript-specific enrichment and directional library construction.
