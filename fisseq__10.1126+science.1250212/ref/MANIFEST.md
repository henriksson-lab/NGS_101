# FISSEQ — third-party reference material

Third-party files are deliberately not committed. The model was checked against:

- Lee *et al.*, “Highly multiplexed subcellular RNA sequencing in situ,” *Science*
  (2014), doi:[10.1126/science.1250212](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1250212), PMCID
  **PMC4140943**. This is the defining report.
- Lee *et al.*, “Fluorescent in situ sequencing (FISSEQ) of RNA for gene expression
  profiling in intact cells and tissues,” *Nature Protocols* (2015),
  doi:[10.1038/nprot.2014.191](https://doi.org/10.1038/nprot.2014.191), PMCID
  **PMC4327781**. This open protocol supplies the exact RT, RCA and five sequencing
  primers and the detailed reaction order.

Run `python3 tools/get_sources.py "FISSEQ"`. Files belong under
`$CHEM_DATA/sources/fisseq__10.1126+science.1250212/`, never here.
