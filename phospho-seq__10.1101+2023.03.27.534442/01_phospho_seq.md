# Phospho-seq

**Defining work:** Chung et al., doi:10.1101/2023.03.27.534442 (preprint 2023; peer-reviewed article available from PMCID PMC11794950).

## Molecular boundary

🟢 Fixed, permeabilized and hashed cells are stained with DNA-tagged antibodies against intracellular total and phosphorylated proteins. Bridge oligos make TotalSeq-A and TotalSeq-B tags compatible with 10x ATAC gel-bead capture.

🟢 After GEM barcoding, the first SPRI cleanup separates ATAC DNA on the beads from antibody and hashing tags in the supernatant. Tag formats are amplified separately while retaining the droplet cell barcode and UMI.

## Evidence boundary

🟢 Sources checked: the defining bioRxiv article and methods supplement, the peer-reviewed article's JATS text, the available antibody-index workbook, and the cited ASAP-seq article and supplement (JATS line 234; Supplementary Methods lines 250–258). The Phospho-seq methods print the home-conjugated TotalSeq-style tag architectures, blocking oligo, P5 and two P7 index-primer families. The exact BOA and BOB sequences are inherited from ASAP-seq rather than reprinted by Phospho-seq. Several other published supplemental files and two author-linked step-by-step PDFs remain inaccessible; the source review records their links and status.

🟢 Bridge oligo A (BOA), 5′→3′: `TCGTCGGCAGCGTCAGATGTGTATAAGAGACAGNNNNNNNNNVTTTTTTTTTTTTTTTTTTTTTTTTTTTTTT/3InvdT/`. Its `N9V` window is the unique bridging identifier for TotalSeq-A tags.

🟢 Bridge oligo B (BOB), 5′→3′: `TCGTCGGCAGCGTCAGATGTGTATAAGAGACAGTTGCTAGGACCGGCCTTAAAGC/3InvdT/`. The 3′ block makes each bridge a template rather than an extendable primer.

🟢 Tag-library PCR uses P5 `AATGATACGGCGACCACCGA` and one of two indexed P7 families: TotalSeq-A `CAAGCAGAAGACGGCATACGAGATXXXXXXXXGTGACTGGAGTTCCTTGGCACCCGAGAATTCCA`, or TotalSeq-B `CAAGCAGAAGACGGCATACGAGATXXXXXXXXGTGACTGGAGTTCAGACGTGTGC`, where `XXXXXXXX` is the chosen i7 index.

🟢 The peer-reviewed supplement also supplies the antibody-specific 15-nt barcode table. Those target-dependent barcodes are source data, not a single universal primer sequence, and remain represented as a feature-barcode window on the schematic.

🟡 The run is stated as 34 bp Read 1, 8 bp i7, 16 bp i5 and 34 bp Read 2. The tag-library cell-barcode end is generated by the versioned commercial 10x scATAC gel-bead chemistry; the public paper does not print that bead oligo, so a complete base-by-base four-primer landing model is not asserted.
