# eTIP-seq — source-qualified partial preparation

[Topoisomerase IIβ targets DNA crossovers formed between distant homologous sites to induce chromatin opening](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-020-75004-w), Miyaji et al., Scientific Reports 10, 18550 (29 October 2020).

Two published originals inspected: PMC7596052 main Introduction/Results preparation context, Methods164–176 and computational filtering188–190; actual main Fig3 PDFp5 and modified adaptor orders PDFp11; published Supplementary Information MOESM6 notes on methodology p1 and actual FigS1/caption p2. The whole external bundle was scanned with the mandatory primer scraper. This is a private partial preparation, not a complete molecular, source or absence audit. Third-original budget remains unused.

🟢 Etoposide traps an enzymatic intermediate of topoIIβ at DNA G-segments. T-segments can remain associated noncovalently. This is not formaldehyde ChIP and does not inherit CC-seq silica/TDP2 chemistry. The study uses two complementary routes, eTIPa and eTIPb, within one method. Distinct independently prepared route inputs are not created by pretending one IP stock was experimentally split between a and b.

🟢 Selected starting material is prepared day2 cerebellar granule neuron culture in100mm dishes. Treat with0.5mM etoposide15min, lyse with1%sarkosyl/pass23G needle, addCsCl to0.5M, microprobe-sonicate, dilute with3volumes TEST100 (Tris7.5/EDTA/100mMNaCl/Triton/protease inhibitors). Source supernatant is captured overnight4°C with anti-topoIIβ3B6 on ProteinG magnetic beads, then washed3×TEST200. Upstream animal/culture procedures remain outside the graph. Exact stock volumes, occupancy, fragmentation yield and nonselected material are unassigned.

🟢 eTIPa treats the washed preparation with TEST500 at4°C: the P2 eluate separates physically from bead-associated P1. Both are independently digested with RNaseA/proteinaseK, extracted with phenol/chloroform and precipitated with ethanol plus molecular carrier glycogen, then Covaris-sheared to~200bp. FigS1p2 reports485ng DNA for each fraction's sequencing preparation; amount supplied to a kit reaction and any intermediate dilution/stock remainder are not specified. Source TruSeqDNA LT library preparation is represented as an aggregate operation without importing a current vendor recipe. TenPCRcycles, BioAnalyzerQC and HiSeq2000PE100 are explicit. P1/P2 libraries remain separate; the graph does not invent an experimental pool.

P1 is mostly covalently retained G-segment DNA, not a pure species. The source states resealed G-segments can be recovered in P2 along with T-segments; P2 is not synonymous with pure transferred-segment DNA. Source sonication was deliberately limited to preserve noncovalent association. Inter-complex chimeras and incomplete retention remain possible.

🟢 Selected eTIPb bypasses TEST500 fractionation. On the washed IP beads, T4DNApolymerase blunts sheared ends and a source NEBNext module with Klenow performs3′dA-tailing. Whole packaging/doses not stated by these names are not invented. Ligate the source adaptor at1.5µM with12,000U T4DNA ligase/5%PEG8000/protease inhibitors/NEB buffer for6h22°C, then phosphorylate adaptor5′ends with T4PNK. A second, fresh T4DNA-ligase reaction joins adaptors overnight16°C with rotation. Stop on ice with10mMEDTA. The source does not supply a complete end-occupancy or sealed-molecule map. The PNK output includes a separate inventory view of the same copresent adaptor joining ends for the second ligation; this is bookkeeping within one reaction, not a fresh adaptor dose or physical separation.

Whole written adaptor orders, main174/actualPDFp11:

- Top: `5′-GTTGGATCCGATA[Bio-dT]CGC-3′`.
- Bottom: `5′-GGCCGCGATATCGGATCCAACT-3′`.

🟢 `[Bio-dT]` is one physical thymidine nucleotide carrying biotin, not a linker replacing the base. The top backbone has17nt; the bottom has22nt. 🟡 Computation from the literal orders gives a17bp core, a bottom3′T compatible with source DNA3′A, and a bottom5′GGCC overhang; GGCC is self-reverse-complementary for the second joining step. The local Scene checks pairing. The prepared adaptor duplex and its actual ligation allocation/untransferred stock retain the same known whole orders, local geometry and internal Bio-dT metadata; an aliquot does not erase known reagent identity. Source Fig3p5 distinguishes5′P/5′OH marks schematically; the local free-ordering view does not assign undisclosed ordered terminal modifications from an anonymous mark. PNK's intended phosphorylated end class is retained without claiming every end reacted. Full attached/chimeric molecules, nicks, occupancies and whole adapter-repair chemistry remain unassembled.

🟢 After second ligation, recover bead-bound DNA using the stated eTIPa RNaseA/PK/phenol-chloroform/glycogen-ethanol procedure. T4DNApolymerase with dATP/dTTP removes biotinylated nucleotides from terminal adaptors as the source describes. This is not general cleavage of a biotin linker or global removal of every biotin; exact processed sequences/byproducts remain unassembled. Covaris re-fragmentation to~200bp precedes C1streptavidin capture and3×Tween wash. Internal adaptor-associated biotin supports the intended enrichment; other retained/unreacted classes remain unresolved.

🟢 Bead-bound fragments are end-repaired, dA-tailed and ligated to paired-end sequencing adapters. FifteenPCRcycles use an actual Illumina PCR master mix and actual primer cocktail: master enzyme/nucleotides/buffer and two primer classes are components of those existing physical supplies, not added fresh duplicate doses. Exact historical kit adapter/primer bodies and index assignment remain unassembled. AMPurecleanup, actual BioAnalyzerQC aliquot and physical sequencing allocation lead to HiSeq2000PE100. No unreported pooling ratio, reagent volume or read landing is supplied.

Main188–190 filtering removes reads starting with ligation-adaptor sequence and selects uniquely mapped pairs computationally; this does not physically eliminate molecules from the prepared library. The source's intra/inter-complex inference and genomic chimera distance are not an atom map, a physical DNA length or a UMI. SP120ChIP, FAIRE, mRNA-seq and imaging controls remain separate branches.
