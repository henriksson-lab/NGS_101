# ReDeeM — research notes

Defining source: Weng *et al.*, [10.1038/s41586-024-07066-z](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-07066-z).

## Evidence used

- 🟢 ReDeeM modifies droplet-based 10x Multiome using whole cells and conditions that retain mitochondrial DNA while preserving RNA and ATAC libraries.
- 🟢 Tiled mtDNA probes hybrid-capture a third, deeply sequenced library carrying the same cell barcodes.
- 🟢 Cell barcode plus mtDNA fragment endpoints act as endogenous molecular identifiers for consensus error correction.
- 🟢 The article's [Supplementary Data 1](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10937407/) is an XLSX with a `Primers` sheet and eight named mtDNA-probe sheets (four human and four mouse). It publishes the individual capture-probe sequences and the `Nextera_N701`–`Nextera_N706`, P5, P7 and `Hash-Spikin primer` sequences. The full probe inventory belongs in that source workbook, not a copied table here.
- 🟢 The separate supplementary protocol specifies four staggered probe pools for each mtDNA capture, post-capture PCR with P5/P7, and paired-end sequencing of the mtDNA branch (R1 150, i7 8, i5 24, R2 150 cycles). It also prints optional HTO-additive and Hash-primer oligos and a generic `N701`–`N712` primer pattern.
- 🟡 The published supplements disagree: the ReDeeM protocol (extracted lines 64–65) says probe sequences were forthcoming and describes four sets of 137 100-nt probes; Supplementary Methods (lines 456–465) describe 138 biotinylated 120-nt oligos per set. Supplementary Data 1 instead gives the individual probe bases, mostly 120 nt, with varying row counts across all eight human/mouse sheets and a few shorter human v1 rows. The page uses that workbook for sequence identities; its plain base strings do not independently establish synthesis modifications or per-sample pool assignment.
- 🟢 The exact cited [10x CG000338 Rev B guide mirrored as its original PDF](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Teichlab/scg_lib_structs/master/data/10X-Genomics/CG000338_ChromiumNextGEM_Multiome_ATAC_GEX_User_Guide_RevB.pdf), Appendix “Sequences” (locally extracted lines 5659–5705), prints the fixed RNA and ATAC gel-bead oligo scaffolds and final library shells. Thus the fixed commercial arms are published, even though the ReDeeM supplement names them only by reagent.
- 🟢 The ReDeeM Supplementary Methods (lines 433–440) cite CG000338 **Rev A** for GEM generation and RNA library preparation, while the separate author protocol (lines 144–156 and 416) cites **Rev B** for those steps. Both guides are now available: their Sequences appendices (Rev A lines 5632–5688; Rev B lines 5659–5712) print identical fixed bead arms and final ATAC/GEX layouts. Which revision was followed in each experiment and any procedural differences remain unresolved.
- 🟢 Model correction (bounded pass 2026-10-17): all three final libraries were previously generic shells, and the mtDNA library was drawn as a TruSeq library with a dA junction and a 10-base cell barcode inside the insert. The mtDNA library is captured from the indexed ATAC library, so it now uses the CG000338 Rev A ATAC layout: `5′-AATGATACGGCGACCACCGAGATCTACAC-N16-CGCGTCTG-TCGTCGGCAGCGTCAGATGTGTATAAGAGACAG-insert-CTGTCTCTTATACACATCTCCGAGCCCACGAGAC-N8-ATCTCGTATGCCGTCTTCTGCTTG`. Here the i7 N8 is the ReDeeM Nextera N70X index rather than a 10x sample index. The RNA library uses the Rev A gene-expression layout: P5 arm `AATGATACGGCGACCACCGAGATCT`, N10 i5, TruSeq Read 1, 16-base barcode, 12-base UMI, T30VN, cDNA, TruSeq Read 2, N10 i7, P7.
- 🟢 Supplementary Methods ("Single-cell joint RNA and ATAC library preparation", extracted lines 431–451): the pre-amplified product was eluted in 100 µl. A 35 µl aliquot went to RNA library Steps 6–7. For the ATAC library, 8 × 50 µl PCRs each used 5 µl pre-amp, NEBNext HF master mix, 2.4 µl 10 µM Nextera N70X (Supplementary Data 1), 2.4 µl 10x SI PCR Primer B (PN-2000128) and SYBR Green, for 6–12 cycles, followed by SPRI 0.4×–1.55× (2,000–4,000 ng). The SI PCR Primer B sequence is not printed in the inspected sources, so that PCR's geometry is not reconstructed.
- 🟢 Supplementary Data 1 `Primers` sheet: N70X = `CAAGCAGAAGACGGCATACGAGAT[8-nt]GTCTCGTGGGCTCGG` with indexes N701 TCGCCTTA, N702 CTAGTACG, N703 TTCTGCCT, N704 GCTCAGGA, N705 AGGAGTCC, N706 CATGCCTA; P5 `AATGATACGGCGACCACCGAGA`; P7 `CAAGCAGAAGACGGCATACGAGAT`; `Hash-Spikin primer` `GTGACTGGAGTTCAGACGTGTGCTCTTCCGAT*C*T`.
- 🟢 Capture (Supplementary Methods lines 453–470): for each probe set, 500 ng ATAC library is combined with Cot DNA and purified by SPRI, then eluted in hybridization buffer, enhancer, probe set and xGen Universal Blockers NXT; 95 °C 30 s, then 65 °C 16 h. Streptavidin Dynabeads capture and xGen washes follow on day 2. The washed beads go straight into 3–5 cycles of KAPA HiFi PCR with P5/P7, and the four reactions are pooled and purified with 1.6× SPRI.
- 🟡 Published discrepancy in read lengths: Supplementary Methods say all three libraries were sequenced on NovaSeq S4 300-cycle kits as Read1 149, i7 8, i5 24, Read2 149. The protocol recommends R1 150, i7 8, i5 24, R2 150 for ATAC/mtDNA and R1 28, i7 10, i5 10, R2 90 for RNA. The page uses 149/8/24/149 for the ATAC and mtDNA libraries and assigns no RNA cycle counts.
- 🟡 Remaining modelling limits: the pre-amplification and RNA Steps 6–7 are warned boundaries, because the 10x guide does not print those primers. The Tn5 transposon adapters are named only by reagent. PCR duplexes are not reconstructed.
- 🟢 Supplementary Methods lines 453–470 specify four **biotinylated** mtDNA-probe pools, hybridization to the ATAC library, streptavidin-bead capture and post-capture PCR. The [current IDT xGen Hybridization and Wash v2 guide](https://sfvideo.blob.core.windows.net/sitefinity/docs/default-source/protocol/xgen-hybridization-capture-of-dna-libraries-protocol.pdf?sfvrsn=9da6e007_29) corroborates the 5′-biotin/streptavidin mechanism; its 2025 revision does not establish which vendor protocol revision the 2024 study used.
- 🟢 Supplementary Methods lines 441–464 identify the **amplified, indexed ATAC library product** as the mtDNA-hybridization input (500 ng for each of four probe pools). The graph therefore splits that library into an ATAC sequencing aliquot and a mtDNA capture aliquot; mtDNA is not a separate product before ATAC indexing.
- 🟢 The defining article's CRISPR lineage-tracing extension (JATS Methods, “CRISPR lineage-tracing experiment with ReDeeM”) prints `oDYT023`–`oDYT038` target-site PCR primer templates with Illumina-compatible arms and variable sample indices. Those primers make an **additional target-site library** for that experiment; they are not the mtDNA capture probes or the standard ReDeeM ATAC/GEX index oligos. Their presence must not be mistaken for a missing core ReDeeM primer inventory.
- 🟢 The defining article cites Lareau *et al.*'s mtscATAC-seq and Mimitou *et al.*'s DOGMA-seq for the whole-cell fixation/permeabilization premise. The DOGMA original reports mtDNA retention after mild permeabilization, while its antibody bridge oligos belong to a different branch and are not part of ReDeeM. The full Lareau predecessor and its linked methods remain a source-review blocker in the current bundle.

## Protocol boundary

ReDeeM captures mtDNA from Multiome material; MAESTER instead enriches mitochondrial transcripts from scRNA-seq cDNA.
