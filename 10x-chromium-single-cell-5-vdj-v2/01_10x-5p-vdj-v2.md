# 10x Chromium Single Cell 5' V(D)J v2 — source note

The combined source audit, exact V(D)J primer pools, and derivations are retained in the
[10x 5' V(D)J research note](../10x-chromium-single-cell-5-vdj/01_chromium-5-vdj.md).

**Primary source:** 10x Genomics, *Chromium Next GEM Single Cell 5' Reagent Kits v2
(Dual Index) User Guide*, **CG000331 Rev F**. This page represents the v2 construct with
a 16-nt cell barcode and 10-nt UMI.


## Linked source scope (2026-10-16)

🟢 The original Dual Index Kit TT Set A CSV is available from the manufacturer
and contains 96 well-to-index pairs, with the two instrument-specific i5
orientations. SI-TT-A1 has i7 `GTAACATGCG`, i5 workflow A `AGTGTTACCT`, and
i5 workflow B `AGGTAACACT`. These are sequencing/sample-sheet values.

The source audit also opened the guide-linked CG00053 cell-preparation
guide (Rev C and the newer Rev D handbook), CG000126 counting flowchart,
CG000123 CD3-cell enrichment, CG000479 chip-failure note and CG000396
instrument guide (Rev M). These govern cell preparation or instrument
operation before the molecular workflow. CG00053 Rev D explicitly excludes
nuclei from V(D)J; this schematic starts from cells. CG000123 describes an
optional CD3-positive input-cell enrichment, not V(D)J cDNA enrichment PCR.
CG000396 Rev M is later than the chemistry guide and is used solely to
confirm the instrument-only scope, not to assign a 2024 reagent sequence.
Their additional sample-specific preparation, flow-sorting, counting and
instrument-network references were not imported into the library construct.
Independent-review status and exact locations remain in the source checklist.
