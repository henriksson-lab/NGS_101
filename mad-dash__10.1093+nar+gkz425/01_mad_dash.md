## Published source wording and unresolved chemistry

The main calls the3′adapter preadenylated. The published SI1135–1145 instead places its adenylation at the **3′end** and its3-carbon spacer at the **5′end**. That wording appears reversed relative to the conventional ligation-compatible5′adenylate/3′block assignment. The complete authoritative ordering/end chemistry was not verified within this pass. The model retains the literal source wording, records the conventional assignment only as an inferred hypothesis, and leaves ligation geometry explicitly unavailable. It does not silently repair the publication or render either terminal assignment as established. The source-known ordering body remains distinct from this unresolved end chemistry.

The SI also starts its MAD-DASH reaction at20µl, adds1µl RNaseA and1µl ProteinaseK, then specifies30µl of PCR2 additions while its cleanup refers to50µl PCR products. Exact transfer/allocation is unreviewed; no unreported2µl removal or normalization to a52µl bead ratio is invented. This quantity question does not establish absent disclosure. Input masses, RTanneal schedules, stock formulations and normalization recommendations of main versus reproduced SI remain individually scoped rather than declared erroneous merely because they differ.

The selected-break helper conserves both pieces of caller-supplied DNA and is inferred by default. It does not predict Cas9 cut offsets, PAM recognition, binding, yields or amplifiability across an unassembled library. Whole RNA/RT/adapter/junction/PCR/read geometry, guide-stock molecular inventory, direct Roberts2015/Alon2011 predecessor originals and authoritative modified-adapter ordering records remain deferred.
