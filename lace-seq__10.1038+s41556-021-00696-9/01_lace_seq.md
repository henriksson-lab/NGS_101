The defining original was retrieved as publisher HTML, but its accessible body exposes the abstract, references and extended-data captions rather than the full Methods. The abstract describes RBP-mediated reverse-transcription termination and linear amplification of cDNA ends. This is a method identity, not authority to reconstruct the reaction sequence from other CLIP methods.
