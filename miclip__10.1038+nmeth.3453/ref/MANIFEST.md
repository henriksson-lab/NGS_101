# Source manifest

- Defining paper: https://doi.org/10.1038/nmeth.3453
- Detailed open protocol: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5562447/
- The detailed protocol's section 2.3 prints the L3 linker, cut oligo, PCR primers and sixteen RT primers. Fetch with `python3 tools/get_sources.py --doi 10.1007/978-1-4939-6807-7_5`.
- Cited predecessor: König et al. 2010, https://doi.org/10.1038/nsmb.1838; its original [Supplementary Methods PDF](https://media.springernature.com/original/springer-static/esm/art%3A10.1038%2Fnsmb.1838/MediaObjects/41594_2010_BFnsmb1838_MOESM21_ESM.pdf) still needs full retrieval. The exact repeatable routes are also in `catalogue/extra_sources.tsv`.
- Same-group detailed pre-miCLIP iCLIP protocol: Huppertz et al. 2014, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ymeth.2013.10.011; [author-hosted original PDF](https://edoc.mdc-berlin.de/id/eprint/21794/1/21794oa.pdf) prints L3-App and Rtclip oligo families on pp. 2–3. It is family evidence, not direct 2015 sample attribution.
