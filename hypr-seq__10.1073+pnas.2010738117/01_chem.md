🟢 Adjacent RNA-targeted initiators recruit B1H1 and an adapted B1H2 readout, followed by readout-to-5′-initiator ligation. The reporter DNA is sequenced; the RNA itself is not copied by reverse transcription or directly sequenced. This is not the conventional repeated HCR chain used by the separate smFISH comparator.

🟢 The5′ initiator is5′-phosphorylated `GGAGGGCAGCAAACGG` +`AA` +25nt target homology +10N UMI +`CTCGACCGTTAGCAAAGCTC`. The3′ initiator is25nt target homology +`TA` +`GAAGAGTCTTCCTTTACG`. For a supplied52nt target reference, the3′ probe reverse-complements positions1–25; the5′ probe reverse-complements28–52, leaving2nt unbound. `SelectedProbePair` computes hypothetical source-layout primers from explicit caller DNA and defaults to inferred; it is not inserted as an actual experimental panel.

🟢 Source whole B1H1: `CGTAAAGGAAGACTCTTCCCGTTTGCTGCCCTCCTCGCATTCTTTCTTGAGGAGGGCAGCAAACGGGAAGAG`. Readout: `CTTACGGATGTTGCACCAGCAAGAAAGAATGCGA`. Bead oligo:5′-bead-linker-PC-linker-`CAAGCAGAAGACGGCATACGAGATJJJJJJJJJJJJGTTGGCACCAGGCTTACGGATGTTGCACCAGC`-3′. Twelve Js denote the bead identifier, not a molecule UMI. UMI meaning is one hybridization/ligation event; multiple probes per RNA do not guarantee one UMI per native RNA.

🟢 Fix4%formaldehyde1h; PBST washes;70%EtOH4°C10min; washes/prehybridize;20nM initiators37°Covernight; four10min washes. B1H1 and readout are separately95°C90s/30minRT-cooled, then added **sequentially**,75nM37°C1h each with intervening washes. T4ligase1hRT; PBSTwash/filter. Count cells. Load2000cells/20000beads/indexing primer500nM/EvaGreen into25ul; **only20ul** enters cartridge with70uloil. UV365nm5min releases bead primers; droplets undergo30PCRcycles. The graph's known compound operation consumes already-co-loaded stocks once; it does not add primers again merely to satisfy typed-PCR ports. Unused cell stock and5ul premix remain physical remainders. Default is source-allowed one-well breakage; two-to-four-well pooling is a separate option. PFO breakage recovers top aqueous phase;1.8×SPRI, gelQC and Qubit follow. Gel206bp check is analytical, not invented preparative gel extraction.
