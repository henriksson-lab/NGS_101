# ChAR-seq — research notes

## Construct path

🟢 Crosslinked chromatin-associated RNA is fragmented and ligated in situ to the RNA-compatible end of a biotinylated bridge. The bridge’s opposite end is joined to nearby genomic DNA. RNA is converted to cDNA, material is fragmented and bridge-bearing junctions are affinity enriched before paired-end library construction. The internal bridge provides the orientation used to distinguish RNA-derived from DNA-derived sequence.
