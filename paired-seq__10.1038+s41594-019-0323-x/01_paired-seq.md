# Paired-seq — source intake

Defining paper: Zhu et al., *Nature Structural & Molecular Biology* (2019), DOI
[10.1038/s41594-019-0323-x](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41594-019-0323-x).

🟢 RNA-derived cDNA and tagmented chromatin remain in the nucleus through four rounds of
combinatorial barcode ligation, then separate into sequencing-library branches.
