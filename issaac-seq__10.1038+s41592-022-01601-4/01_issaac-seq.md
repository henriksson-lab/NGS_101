## Construct path

🟢 The RNA arm begins with a 5′-biotinylated TruSeq-bearing 10-base-UMI oligo(dT). Accessible chromatin is tagged with Tn5. The paper describes both FACS plate and droplet implementations; the page shows their common molecular operation and the final RNA and ATAC libraries separately. 🟢 Supplementary Table 4 supplies the exact modified ME-bottom and RNA capture oligos.
