# MSR-seq — source-qualified partial preparation

[Defining paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-022-30261-3), *A multiplex platform for small RNA sequencing elucidates multifaceted tRNA stress response and translational regulation*.

Three published originals inspected: PMC9072684 main49–65/165–203/301 and actualFig1p3, SI1Figure1 controls, and actualData5 MOESM8 oligo workbook. This is a private partial model, not complete reaction or source-absence review.

🟢 The capture-hairpin oligo (CHO) contains5′phosphate/rA, a sample barcode, internal biotin-dT, an embedded hairpin primer-binding site and3′rU/phosphate. Initial RNA3′ligation/barcoding is followed by streptavidin capture and optional pooling. CIP exposes the hairpin3′OH for on-bead self-primedRT; RNaseH removesRNA. Periodate blocks the unextendedCHO's terminalrU while DNA-ended extendedcDNA survives as a second-ligation substrate. SourceFig1d/SI1d explicitly test deoxy-ended ligation. The physical retained-hairpin/bead route differs from free-adapter/circle preparations; sample count or stress panels alone are not methods.

🟢 Select standard deacylated totalRNA, not β-elimination/CMC/AlkB:100mMTris9/37°C30min, neutralize180mMNaOAc4.8 and select ethanol cleanup. Up to1µgRNA in50µl firstligation:1UµlRnl1,buffer,15%PEG8000,50µMATP,1mMhexaamminecobaltchloride,5%DMSO,1µMCHO;16°C12+h. Add equal water; source12µlC1 per50pmolCHO example/15minRT, remove supernatant/highsalt+low-saltwash. Pool individually barcoded retained beads when selected; no invented common precursor or volume/equimolar rule.

RNaseH50µl.4Uµl/37°C15min, wash/resuspend40µl. Add10µl250mMperiodate/.5MNaOAc5,RT30min;167mMribosequench5min;wash. Stored4°C/−20°C alternatives need not be actual fate of the selected trace.

PCR products are off-bead; actual residual beads and selected library pool/loading stocks remain separate.
