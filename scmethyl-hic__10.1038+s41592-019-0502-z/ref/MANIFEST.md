# scMethyl-HiC source files

Run `python3 tools/get_sources.py --slugs scmethyl-hic__10.1038+s41592-019-0502-z` to obtain the original Li et al. 2019 article, the Springer Supplementary Protocol and PMC copies, and the linked 2022 IDT xGen Adaptase successor guide. Files and extracted `.txt` twins live in `$CHEM_DATA/sources/scmethyl-hic__10.1038+s41592-019-0502-z/` and are not committed. The original supplementary protocol is the authority for this page's oligos; the 2022 guide is a later chemistry comparison.
