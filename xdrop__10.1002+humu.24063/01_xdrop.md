## Source-positive workflow

🟢 About4ng genomicDNA enters the TP53 double-emulsion PCR branch. Source primers are `GGTGTGATGGGATGGATAAA` and `CCCTGCATTTCTTTTGTTTG`. The source dPCR mix is Samplix2×; after94°C2min, perform40cycles94°C3s/60°C30s. The model does not assign the complete genomic binding sites.

🟢 TP53 uses a DNA-intercalating DropletStain afterPCR. This differs from the HPV18 hydrolysis-probe branch; the model does not mix that probe with TP53. FACS enriches fluorescent double-emulsion droplets. All their carried DNA remains, including short detector products and unassigned co-encapsulated material. Positive gating is not perfect molecular target specificity.

🟢 Coalesce collected droplets with20µl PicoBreak, mix/centrifuge and transfer3–10µl aqueousphase into droplet MDA. The published Results explicitly identifies Phi29; Methods describes a supplied Samplix mixture, whose complete formulation is unassembled. Incubate30°C16h then65°C10min. Coalesce again and transfer about6µl into the second droplet-MDA round. Coalesce after that round too. The graph retains aliquot remainders with unresolved fates and does not assume that MDA selectively erases or cannot amplify detector-derived DNA.

## Sequencing boundary

🟢 TP53 material was used for a5kb SMRTbellTemplatePrepKit1.0 library from1µg enrichedDNA, sequenced on PacBioRSII with C4 chemistry/P6polymerase and240min movie. CCS computation follows sequencing; it does not physically ligate fragments or preserve their input modifications. This kit preparation/adapter topology is not assembled in the graph, which stops at enriched amplifiedDNA.

Separate source HPV18 routes use PacBio, Eurofins Illumina2×150, or ONT SQK-RAD003/MIN106R9.4; these are not interleaved into the TP53 recipe. Superseded preprint10.1101/409086 was not used.
