# Capture and ligation animation source review

Reviewed 1 November 2026. This is a selected mechanism audit, not a complete
missing-information/absence review. Movie geometry and specimen choices are
illustrative. Existing protocol definitions supply source sequences and graph steps.
No whole-protocol validation, yield, or published absence claim is introduced.

## CRISPR-MIP

Source bundle `crispr-mip__10.1101+2024.03.28.587082`:
`2024.03.28.587082_v1.full.pdf.txt`, lines458–493, probe design and four-step capture
Methods; `2024.03.28.587082_media-2.xlsx.txt`, sheet primers & probes, rows1–8.
The defining catalogue version is this preprint; no later publication is inferred.
Read exact arm orders, 112bp capture, joint Phusion HF/Ampligase incubation, source
Exonuclease I EN0581 and Exonuclease III M0206, and TableS2 probe5′phosphate/UMI/index.
Scraper log `/tmp/capture-crispr-primer-scan.log` was checked against original text.
The already documented Methods134bp versus TableS2 126nt discrepancy stays explicit;
TableS2 supplies the model probe. The source's full guide-cassette example is an
illustrative lentiCRISPRv2 member; no study-wide genotype or genomic clearance is claimed.
The simulator follows source-qualified accessible ends and retains ExoI's5′dinucleotide,
ExoIII's remaining target base and all released nucleotide identities. Closed-circle
resistance is limited to the declared end-dependent activities, not all nuclease actions.

## Small-seq

Source bundle `small-seq__10.1038+nbt.3701`:
`hagemann_s41596-018-0049-y.pdf.txt`, lines334–365 (adapter ligation/free-adapter
processing) and651–680 (modified RA3/RA5/RTP orders); published detailed2018 protocol
for the named Small-seq method. Scraper log `/tmp/capture-smallseq-primer-scan.log`.
Read source preadenylated DNA RA3 with terminal physical ddC, ATP-free T4 RNA
ligase2 truncated KQ, free-adapter deadenylation to5′P, biotin-protected RTP, lambda
exonuclease, and ATP-dependent T4 RNA ligase1 joining the amino-blocked RNA RA5.
The eight H-class RNA UMI bases remain A/C/U classes. The22N small-RNA input is an
illustrative length, not a source specimen. The explicit strict duplex/DNA digestion
model stops at RA3's unpaired ddC; its residual1nt is a selected substrate-boundary
illustration, not a measured final fragment or an assertion that source cleanup is
incomplete. RNA masking, RT, PCR and native cleanup efficiency stay outside the movie.

## In-situ Hi-C

Source bundle `in-situ-hi-c__10.1016+j.cell.2014.11.021`:
`Rao-3D-map-human-genome-cell-2014-extended.pdf.txt`, printedS31,
lines6661–6678, steps12–17, cross-checked against the ENCODE transcription
`Hi-C_Protocol.pdf.txt`, lines52–84: MboI, inactivation, biotin-14-dATP
plus dCTP/dGTP/dTTP, Klenow and T4 DNA ligase. The original article's title/DOI and both source identities were verified against the bundle manifest. Scraper log `/tmp/capture-hic-primer-scan.log`.
Source restriction geometry comes from the protocol-owned MboI definition.
Two synthetic32bp loci demonstrate both staggered cuts, cohesive-contact dissociation,
both selected-end fill-ins, physical biotin incorporation, blunt-end approximation,
and both covalent joins. The GATCGATC junction is generated by the simulator and
compared with the protocol definition. Two non-selected duplex fragments remain
visible. Proximity motion is geometry only, not sequence-driven annealing or a
contact-probability prediction. Crosslinks, shearing and capture are outside the movie.
