## Physical chemistry versus deletion readout

TBIA bifunctional2′OH acylation links two actual proximal RNA nucleotides; single-end hydrolysis can also yield monoadducts. IA creates monoadduct controls, with DMSO/no-reagent controls separate. All four nucleotide types can carry selected2′OH reactions. Source RT-C8 traverses selected crosslinks, producing cDNA deletions; the physical RNA is **not deleted** by an RT jump. Background cDNA deletions also occur in IA/no-reagent samples, so a deletion is not proof of a crosslink.

The local RNA constructor retains exact supplied RNA, crosslink and monoadduct identities. Its complementary DNA function receives a strictly descending explicit template-coordinate path. This is a selected-copy relation, not a prediction of jump boundaries, non-templated additions, exact skipping of crosslink-anchor residues, efficiency, structure or false-positive rate. Background controls can accept the same explicitly selected copy path without asserting a covalent link.
